# Get Real Weight Loss (GRWL) Brand Guide

## Mission

To help women over 50 achieve real, sustainable weight loss through compassion, simplicity, and science, without restriction, shame, or quick fixes.

## Purpose

We guide women to reconnect with their bodies, enjoy real food, and create lasting habits that support a full, vibrant life.

## Vision

A world where women are empowered to age with strength, confidence, and self-compassion, and understand that “weight loss” can be gentle, nourishing, and real.

## Core Message

Weight loss doesn’t have to be harsh, complicated, or temporary. At GRWL, we believe in a compassionate, sustainable approach rooted in **real food, real movement, and real rest.**

## Voice & Tone

Warm, supportive, and shame-free. We speak directly to women 50+, with empathy and encouragement, like a trusted coach and a caring friend rolled into one.

We never promote “diet culture.” Instead, we focus on **progress over perfection** and sustainable, real-life strategies.

### Brand Personas

If our brand voice was to embody a (or a combo) of well known people, they would be.:

*   **Mel Robbins** (no-nonsense, being real)
*   **Oprah** (compassionate giving, guidance, understanding)
*   **Betty White** (self humor realistic),
*   **Jamie Lee Curtis** (owning who and where you are in your life journey)
*   **Helen Mirren** (grace & poise)
*   **Les Brown** (overcoming obstacles and set backs)
*   **Wayne Dwyer and Louise Hay** (inspiring and visionary)

## Language to Avoid

We avoid any language that promotes **diet culture, restriction, or shame.**

### Weight-loss clichés or fad terms:

*   “Lose 10 pounds fast”
*   “Fat-blasting” / “Melt the fat”
*   “Detox” / “Cleanse”
*   “Guilt-free”
*   “Cheat day” / “Cheat meal”
*   “No pain, no gain”
*   “Anti-aging” (we prefer *aging well* or *aging strong*)

### Restrictive or negative language:

*   “Bad foods” / “Good foods”
*   “Burn calories”
*   “Fix your body” / “Get your body back”
*   “Willpower” or “discipline” as the main strategy

### Competitor or trademarked terms to avoid:

*   “Weight Watchers,” “WW,” “Noom,” “Jenny Craig,” “Optavia,” “Nutrisystem,” “Beachbody,” or any other commercial diet program.
*   “Ozempic,” “GLP-1,” or any medication-related phrases that imply endorsement.

## Preferred Language

We prefer language that feels:

*   Empowering, compassionate, real-world, and sustainable
*   Focused on *nourishment*, *balance*, and *long-term health*

Aligned with the message:
*“Get real. Not restrictive.”*

## Visual Style

*   **Imagery**: Natural, real-life imagery of women 50+
*   **Color Palette**: Warm neutrals with earthy greens and soft golds
*   **Brand Colors**: `#FBD9B1`, `#2D5D44`, `#674736`, `#4E83A4`, `#FFB433`, `#0D2945`, `#CB3A01`, `#719949`
*   **Fonts**:
    *   Headers: Rufina
    *   Subheadings: Poppins
    *   Body: Nunito and Cantarell
    *   Decorative Accent: Kauchan Script

### Image Subject Guidance

Images that include a person: The main subject should always be a woman aged 50 or older, but vary her ethnicity and cultural background across images. Alternate between ethnicities such as Hispanic/Latina, African American, Congolese, South African, Indian, East Asian, Middle Eastern, and European. Do not default to a Caucasian woman. Her appearance, clothing, and setting should feel authentic to her background. Keep age diversity within 50–80 years old, and represent different skin tones, hairstyles, and features typical of her heritage.

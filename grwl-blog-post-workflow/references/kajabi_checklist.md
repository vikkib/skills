# GRWL Kajabi Blog Post Checklist

These are the standard steps for drafting, formatting, and publishing every Kajabi blog post in this project. Make sure to follow ALL instructions.

## 1. Title & Headline

- Use a clear, compelling title that matches the post’s theme.
- All titles and headlines, subtitles and subheads are in sentence case (capitalize first word + proper nouns).
- Limit the title to ~60 characters for SEO when possible.

## 2. Blog Structure

- **Hook**: Open with a short, relatable paragraph that grabs attention.
- **Personal tie-in (optional)**: Add a short story, insight, or example to build trust.
- **Main content**: Break into 3–6 sections, each with an H2 subheading.
- **Support points**: Expand bullets or lists into explanatory paragraphs.
- **Solution/insight section**: Show how the GRWL philosophy applies (compassion, sustainability, real food/movement/rest).
- **Bold important words and phrases** for ease of reading.
- **Closing + CTA**: End with encouragement and a clear next step (program link, free guide, or related blog).

## 3. Tag Selection (required for every post)

Select the single most relevant tag for the post from this list:

- `food & mood` -- for posts about the emotional or psychological relationship with food
- `food facts` -- for posts grounded in nutrition science, research, or education
- `food finds` -- for posts featuring specific foods, ingredients, or product recommendations
- `get moving` -- for posts focused on exercise, movement, or strength training
- `mental & emotional weight` -- for posts about mindset, stress, self-compassion, or emotional barriers to weight loss
- `recipe` -- for posts that include or are centred on a recipe
- `mindset` -- for posts focused on beliefs, habits, motivation, or personal growth

Choose the tag that best reflects the post's primary focus. If the post spans multiple categories, pick the dominant one. Include the selected tag and a one-sentence rationale in the deliverables.

## 4. SEO Elements (required for every post)

- **Slug**: Short, lowercase, hyphen-separated (e.g., compassionate-weight-loss).
- **Meta Title (≤60 characters)**: Clear and clickable; should include main keyword.
- **Meta Description (≤160 characters)**: 1–2 sentences summarizing the post, highlighting benefit + keyword.
- **Keyword/Phrase**: Identify 1 main keyword or phrase (e.g., “compassionate weight loss”) and use it naturally in:
    - Title
    - One subheading
    - A few times in the body (1-3% density)

## 5. Formatting & Readability

- Keep paragraphs short: 2–4 lines max.
- Use H2 subheadings for main points; H3s for supporting detail. Do not skip header levels.
- Bold key words/phrases to draw the eye.
- Use bullet points or numbered lists where helpful.
- Include emojis or icons sparingly to add warmth and scannability.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Follow WCAG 2.0 accessibility standards.

## 6. Visuals

Every post requires exactly three images. Generate all three using the `generate_image` tool, using the brand visual style (women 50+, real-life, natural, warm, brand colors `#FBD9B1`, `#2D5D44`, `#674736`, `#4E83A4`, `#FFB433`, `#0D2945`, `#CB3A01`, `#719949`). Vary the woman's ethnicity across images within the same post.

- **Image 1 — Featured image (top of post)**: `landscape` aspect ratio. Wide, lifestyle-oriented scene. Sets the emotional tone of the post. Place at the very top, above the title or immediately below it in Kajabi.
- **Image 2 — Mid-post image (halfway through the post)**: `landscape` aspect ratio. Relates to the section it sits within. Should feel like a natural continuation of the story, not a repeat of Image 1. Place it between the third and fourth H2 sections.
- **Image 3 — Bottom image (end of post, above or near the CTA)**: `portrait` aspect ratio. Warmer, more intimate or motivational in feel. Reinforces the closing message and CTA. Place it just above or alongside the final CTA paragraph.

- **Alt text**: Write 2–3 sentences per image that:
    - Describe the image clearly
    - Connect to the blog's theme/keyword
    - Stay under 360 characters
    - Optimize for AEO and GIO

## 7. Call-to-Action (CTA)

- Always end with a CTA. Examples:
    - Learn more about the Get Real Weight Loss Program
    - Read our related post on [link]
- CTA should be encouraging, not pushy — aligned with our compassionate tone.

## 8. Tone & Voice

- Warm, supportive, shame-free.
- Speak directly to women over 50.
- Emphasize sustainability, self-compassion, and real-life strategies (no quick fixes, no gimmicks).
- Use "we" and "you" for connection.
- **Always use contractions.** The GRWL voice is conversational, not clinical. Write "isn't" not "is not", "can't" not "cannot", "shouldn't" not "should not", "you're" not "you are", "it's" not "it is", "don't" not "do not", "we're" not "we are", "that's" not "that is". Apply this rule to body copy, subheadings, newsletter content, and social posts.

## 9. Final Deliverables Checklist

After creating the post, you must provide:

1.  The selected Kajabi tag and a one-sentence rationale for the selection.
2.  A 150-word summary for schema markup.
3.  An AEO-optimized slug.
4.  A 60-character AEO title.
5.  A 160-character summary.
6.  A 20-word version of the summary for SEO description.
7.  The title in 3 power words, comma-separated.
8.  The main keyword used (must be 1-3% of content).
9.  The final Flesch-Kincaid score (must be 65+).
10. Three images, each with its file path and alt text containing the keyword:
    - Image 1: wide featured image (landscape), placed at the top of the post.
    - Image 2: wide mid-post image (landscape), placed between the third and fourth H2 sections.
    - Image 3: tall closing image (portrait), placed just above or alongside the final CTA.
11. A callout box containing:
    - A short, bold statement (1-2 sentences) capturing the emotional core of the post. Should feel quotable and make the reader pause.
    - A one-sentence CTA linking to the GRWL program or a related resource.
    - Written in contractions, no em dashes.
12. A newsletter summary with:
    - Enticing email subject
    - Headline
    - 140-character preview text
    - Short introductory paragraph
    - Summary of the blog post with a CTA to the `[link to URL]`.
13. Social posts for Facebook and Instagram with relevant hashtags, including `#GetRealWeightLoss`.

## 10. Recipe Post Formatting (use instead of standard Blog Structure for recipe posts)

When the draft is a recipe, replace the standard Blog Structure (Section 2) with the following format. All other sections (Tag Selection, SEO Elements, Formatting, Visuals, Tone, Deliverables) still apply.

### 10.1 Title (H1)

- Sentence case only. Capitalize first word and proper nouns.
- Keep under 60 characters for SEO.
- Must start with the word "Recipe:"

Example: Recipe: Simple gut-friendly salmon bowl

Do not skip header hierarchy.

### 10.2 Hook paragraph (2-4 lines max)

- Short and relatable.
- Speak directly to women 50+.
- Avoid diet culture language.

Example: You want something simple. Something real. Not another complicated plan. This is the kind of meal that supports your body without drama.

### 10.3 Why this recipe supports compassionate weight loss (short paragraph)

- Keep short but meaningful.
- Explain how it supports steady energy, includes real food, aligns with aging strong, and supports compassionate weight loss.
- Bold 2-4 key phrases for scannability.

### 10.4 Ingredients (H2)

- Use a clean bullet list.
- Do NOT change the ingredients provided in the draft.

### 10.5 Instructions (H2)

- Do NOT change the actual instructions provided in the draft.
- Use numbered steps.
- Short sentences. One action per step.

### 10.6 Nutrition

- Do NOT change the nutrition values provided in the draft.
- Present as a bullet point list.

### 10.7 Notes

- Offer simple variations using a bullet list:
    - Swap protein option
    - Make ahead option
    - Budget option
    - Family-friendly tip
- Keep tone warm and practical.

### 10.8 Closing paragraph and CTA

- Encouraging, not pushy.
- Add an internal link to the program page or a related blog post.

Example: You don't need complicated plans. You need real meals that support your life. If you're ready for more compassionate weight loss strategies, explore the Get Real Weight Loss program.

### 10.9 Recipe Image Checklist

Recipe posts require 1 featured image and 2-3 supporting images (instead of the standard 3-image set). All images must follow these rules:

- Women 50+ represented across ethnic backgrounds.
- Natural lighting.
- Real kitchens.
- No staged "diet" vibes.
- Alt text: 2-3 sentences, clear description, includes keyword, under 360 characters.

### 10.10 Recipe SEO Elements

In addition to the standard SEO elements (Section 4), ensure the main keyword appears in:

- The title
- At least one H2
- Body text at 1-3% density
- Meta title
- Meta description

# GRWL Kajabi Blog Post Checklist

These are the standard steps for drafting, formatting, and publishing every Kajabi blog post in this project. Make sure to follow ALL instructions.

## 1. Title & Headline

- Use a clear, compelling title that matches the post’s theme.
- All titles and headlines, subtitles and subheads are in sentence case (capitalize first word + proper nouns).
- Limit the title to ~60 characters for SEO when possible.

## 2. Blog Structure

- **Hook**: Open with a short, relatable paragraph that grabs attention.
- **Personal tie-in (optional)**: Add a short story, insight, or example to build trust.
- **Main content**: Break into 3–6 sections, each with an H2 subheading.
- **Support points**: Expand bullets or lists into explanatory paragraphs.
- **Solution/insight section**: Show how the GRWL philosophy applies (compassion, sustainability, real food/movement/rest).
- **Bold important words and phrases** for ease of reading.
- **Closing + CTA**: End with encouragement and a clear next step (program link, free guide, or related blog).

## 3. SEO Elements (required for every post)

- **Slug**: Short, lowercase, hyphen-separated (e.g., compassionate-weight-loss).
- **Meta Title (≤60 characters)**: Clear and clickable; should include main keyword.
- **Meta Description (≤160 characters)**: 1–2 sentences summarizing the post, highlighting benefit + keyword.
- **Keyword/Phrase**: Identify 1 main keyword or phrase (e.g., “compassionate weight loss”) and use it naturally in:
    - Title
    - One subheading
    - A few times in the body (1-3% density)

## 4. Formatting & Readability

- Keep paragraphs short: 2–4 lines max.
- Use H2 subheadings for main points; H3s for supporting detail. Do not skip header levels.
- Bold key words/phrases to draw the eye.
- Use bullet points or numbered lists where helpful.
- Include emojis or icons sparingly to add warmth and scannability.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Follow WCAG 2.0 accessibility standards.

## 5. Visuals

- **Featured image**: a wide, lifestyle-oriented, photographic image, aligned with GRWL brand (women 50+, real-life, natural, warm, using brand colors).
- **Supporting images**: 2–4 per post where possible.
- **Alt text**: Write 2–3 sentences per image that:
    - Describe the image clearly
    - Connect to the blog’s theme/keyword
    - Stay under 360 characters
    - Optimize for AEO and GIO

## 6. Call-to-Action (CTA)

- Always end with a CTA. Examples:
    - Learn more about the Get Real Weight Loss Program
    - Read our related post on [link]
- CTA should be encouraging, not pushy — aligned with our compassionate tone.

## 7. Tone & Voice

- Warm, supportive, shame-free.
- Speak directly to women over 50.
- Emphasize sustainability, self-compassion, and real-life strategies (no quick fixes, no gimmicks).
- Use “we” and “you” for connection.

## 8. Final Deliverables Checklist

After creating the post, you must provide:

1.  A 150-word summary for schema markup.
2.  An AEO-optimized slug.
3.  A 60-character AEO title.
4.  A 160-character summary.
5.  A 20-word version of the summary for SEO description.
6.  The title in 3 power words, comma-separated.
7.  The main keyword used (must be 1-3% of content).
8.  The final Flesch-Kincaid score (must be 65+).
9.  A wide, photographic image (and its path) with alt text containing the keyword.
10. A newsletter summary with:
    - Enticing email subject
    - Headline
    - 140-character preview text
    - Short introductory paragraph
    - Summary of the blog post with a CTA to the `[link to URL]`.
11. Social posts for Facebook and Instagram with relevant hashtags, including `#GetRealWeightLoss`.
